How it works

Multinomial Naive Bayes adds up the log-probability of every word under "spam" vs "ham" training messages, plus a class prior, then normalizes to a probability. Trained on the public SMS Spam Collection with an 80/20 split. Honest limit: it's a text-only model trained on SMS English, so new scam wording, URLs and sender tricks can slip past it. Treat a "not spam" as no red flags found, not a guarantee.
